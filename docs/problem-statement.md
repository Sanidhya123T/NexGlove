## What is the Problem?

Traditional computer input devices create several limitations:

- **Desk Dependency:** Requires a fixed surface for operation.
- **Restricted Movement:** Limits natural hand movement.
- **Low Portability:** Bulky peripherals are inconvenient to carry.
- **Gaming Limitations:** Conventional controls restrict flexible hand interaction.
- **Limited Customization:** Fixed controls offer less personalization.
- **Multiple Peripherals:** Separate controllers are needed for different devices.
- **Limited Accessibility:** Conventional interfaces can be challenging for users with limited finger mobility or wheelchair users.

### Need

A more natural, portable and wearable way to control computers.
